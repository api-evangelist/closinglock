# Closinglock

> Closinglock is an Austin, Texas fintech and fraud-prevention company whose platform secures the
> money movement and identity checks inside a residential real estate closing. Title companies,
> real estate attorneys, agents and underwriters use it to share wire instructions over an
> authenticated channel instead of email, verify identity and bank-account ownership before funds
> move, collect and disburse funds, retrieve mortgage payoff statements, manage and e-sign closing
> documents, and evidence FinCEN and ALTA Best Practices obligations.

## Important for agents

Closinglock publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-09 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL document, no SDK on any public package registry, no CLI,
no MCP server, and no A2A agent card on any Closinglock host. The subdomains `api.`, `docs.`,
`developer.` and `developers.closinglock.com` do not resolve. Integrations with the title
production systems (SoftPro, RamQuest, ResWare) and with DocuSign are built bilaterally with each
vendor rather than through a self-serve API program. Do not attempt to construct Closinglock API
calls — there is no published contract to ground them in.
